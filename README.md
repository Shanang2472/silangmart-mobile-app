# Silang Mini Mart (Android)

Native Kotlin & Jetpack Compose application for **Silang Mini Mart** branches (`Poblacion` and `Bypass`).

## Core Features
- **Multi-Role Authentication**: Customer login/registration (with store photo & GPS pin), Admin/Cashier (`admin`/`admin` or `lorna`/`1234`), Rider (`rider`/`1234`), and Supplier (`supplier`/`1234`).
- **Admin / Staff Portal**: Overview Dashboard, Cash Register / POS (packaging variants `Tingi`/`Balot`/`Karton`/`Sako`, Senior/PWD 20% discount, 12% VAT inclusive, QR Official Receipt), Multi-branch Stocks & Traceability, GPS Delivery Tracker & Payment Verification, Customer Directory, Value-Added Services (E-Load, Bills Payment, Remittance), Reports & Analytics, and Branch/Staff Settings.
- **Customer Portal**: Browse & order by branch, Delivery Bag checkout, Active/Archived/Trash orders, Live GPS route tracking, Profile & store photo management.
- **Rider & Supplier Portals**: Assigned delivery queue, live GPS fix updates, Proof of Delivery (POD) handover photo, and supplier stock deliveries.
- **Role-Aware AI Assistant**: Tagalog/English shopping, dispatch, and operations co-pilot with voice input and Text-to-Speech.
